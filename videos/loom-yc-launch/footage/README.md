# Drop your footage here

Put your already-edited clips in **this folder**, named exactly as below. `.mp4` (H.264)
is safest; `.mov` / `.webm` also work.

```
videos/loom-yc-launch/footage/
```

A first cut already exists using motion-graphic stand-ins and product stills, so you can
watch the shape before shooting anything. Every clip you drop in replaces a stand-in and
makes that beat more persuasive. **Slots 2 and 3 are the ones that matter** — they're the
hero, and together they're a third of the runtime.

## How to shoot these (matters more than you'd think)

- **Crop tight.** A full 16:9 screenshot of an IDE is unreadable in a 30-second video.
  Frame the region that carries the point — the composer, the baton, the chips.
- **Zoom to ~150%** in the app before recording, or crop in post. If a partner has to
  squint, the shot is doing nothing for you.
- **Speed up dead time.** Nobody needs to watch an agent think for 8 seconds. Cut or
  2–4× any waiting. Keep the *result* landing at full speed.
- **Cursor moves with intent** — one deliberate path, no hunting, no jitter.
- **Kill personal data** — real repo names, tokens, your file tree, notification banners,
  unread badges. Partners notice.
- **Hide system chrome** — macOS menu bar and dock.
- **Shoot a couple of seconds longer** than the slot needs, so there's a choice of beat.
- **Dark theme throughout.** The title cards are near-black with one orange accent; the
  product being dark is what makes the cut feel like one piece.
