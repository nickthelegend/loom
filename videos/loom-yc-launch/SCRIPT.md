# SCRIPT — loom-yc-launch

**Voice:** `am_michael` (Kokoro, local) — male, mid-range, unhurried
**Voice direction:** A developer explaining something to another developer, not an
ad read. Flat and certain. No upward inflection, no enthusiasm on the product name.
The pain in Line 1 should sound *familiar*, not dramatic — the partner watching has
lived it. Let the demo carry the excitement; the voice stays level throughout.

**Total budget:** 68 words / 30s. Roughly 2.3 words per second, which is deliberately
slower than a typical promo — the footage needs room to be read.

---

## Line 1 — The pain (Frame 1)

**Time:** 0.0 – 5.0s
**Delivery:** Deadpan. A statement of fact, not a complaint. Beat before "start over".

    Every coding agent keeps its own memory. Switch tools, and you start over.

## Line 2 — The claim (Frame 2)

**Time:** 5.0 – 8.0s
**Delivery:** Drop in pitch. This is the whole company in six words — land it and stop.

    Loom makes them one brain.

## Line 3 — The mechanism (Frame 3)

**Time:** 8.0 – 17.0s
**Delivery:** The longest line and the most important. Slow down on "the context goes
with it" — that phrase is the product. The three nouns at the end are a list, not a
crescendo; keep them even.

    One thread over every agent. Pass the baton, and the context goes with it —
    decisions, memory, the whole conversation.

## Line 4 — The proof (Frame 4)

**Time:** 17.0 – 22.5s
**Delivery:** Matter-of-fact. "Working today" is the credibility beat — say it plainly,
don't sell it.

    Five agents, working today. Or let Loom route the whole job — plan, execute, review.

## Line 5 — The reach (Frame 5)

**Time:** 22.5 – 26.0s
**Delivery:** Lighter. This is the small surprise.

    Same thread on your desk, and in your pocket.

## Line 6 — Close (Frame 6)

**Time:** 26.0 – 30.0s
**Delivery:** Two beats, full stop between them. Do not read the install command —
it is on screen; saying it aloud is what a bad ad does.

    Loom. One brain, every agent.
