## Loom 1.2.6: safe at scale, chained, shareable

### Your machine stays up
- **A cap on agent turns at once, across every project, and a free-memory floor.** Past either, a new turn waits for a slot and says so in its thread. The default is one turn per 4 GB of RAM, keeping 15% of memory free. `loom limits --turns 2 --min-free 25` changes it live, and a turn that's waited 10 minutes starts anyway.

### Goals across projects, one after another
- **`loom chain plan.txt`** takes one `<project> :: <goal>` per line and runs them in order. Each completed run is scanned for secrets and merged before the next starts. `--push`, `--keep-going` and `--json` are available.
- **`loom orchestrate "…" --apply`** does the same for a single goal.

### Share a thread
- **`loom share [chat]`** gives a teammate a read-only link to a chat: prompts, replies, what ran and what changed. Links expire (24h by default) and can be revoked.

### Orchestras you can script
- **`loom orchestra --json`** and **`loom orchestra <run> --wait`** report documented statuses and give distinct exit codes.
- Runs show tasks by outcome ("9 done · 2 failed · 2 cancelled") instead of "9/13".
- **A task that recovers frees the tasks waiting on it.** Before, they stayed failed.
- A `loom` command that lands during a daemon restart waits for the new daemon.
- antigravity gets 30 minutes a turn by default.
