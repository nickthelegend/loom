## Loom 1.2.3: two people, one goal, both machines

### Team auto-orchestration
- **A goal splits across your teammates' machines on its own.** When a teammate's Loom is a shared runner, your orchestrator sees their agents as `team:codex`, `team:opencode` and so on. Self-contained tasks go to them; tightly coupled ones stay with you.
- **Nobody on their side clicks anything.** Their runner claims the task, runs it with **their** agent in a fresh clone (their subscription, their limits) and pushes the result back.
- **A teammate who goes offline doesn't stall you.** A task nobody takes within 10 minutes goes back to your orchestrator, which hands it to someone else.
- **Still one PR.** Your Loom merges each teammate's task into the goal's integration branch next to your own, checks what it touched against the task's lease, and opens one PR from your machine.
- Set it up once:
  - in the repo: `{ "runners": { "shared": true } }` in `loom.team.json`;
  - each teammate: `loom runner start --shared`;
  - then `loom orchestrate "<goal>"` as usual, or `--solo` to keep a goal on your machine.

### Simple mode, by default
- A project opens on five plain tabs: **Chat, Team, Agents, Board, Memory**. The composer is just a box, the agent and Plan.
- **Developer mode** (Settings → Preferences → Mode) brings back the rest: Orchestra, Crew, Insights, the terminal, console, browser, right-hand panel, and the model, permission and cost chips.
- No Developer vocabulary leaks into Simple mode: no "baton" labels, no permission badges.

### Safer team hub
- When a teammate disconnects, the hub no longer broadcasts their repo names to signed-in users of other teams.
- A teammate can't replace another member's encryption key envelope; only the owner can.
- Old presence, expired leases and invites, and team activity older than 90 days are cleaned up hourly.

### Fixes from the fourth field round
- `loom runner stop` really stops: a stopped runner no longer takes a job the hub pushes to it.
- `loom memory:repair` now shows up in `loom memory` and in what agents are briefed with; add `--dry-run` to preview it.
- A crew card no longer gets stuck asking about its own files.
- Crew checkouts live beside the project on an external disk, like orchestra's.
- `loom crew status` shows card ids, says how to accept a failed card, and has `--json`.
- The Verified card quotes agents' words without raw markdown.
