## Loom 1.3.0: the team workspace

Loom now does what a team agent workspace does: your agents start their own turns, appear in Slack and Discord, and share a running chat with a teammate. They run behind any ACP agent, in a split window, and your phone reaches every computer you run them on. The approach comes from [Nuphos](https://github.com/nuphos/nuphos) (Apache-2.0, © Zeabur Inc., credited in THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md), rebuilt on Loom's local daemon: no hosted backend, nothing to deploy.

**Your agents start their own work**
- **Triggers:** a schedule (`--every 2h`, `--cron "0 9 * * 1-5"`) or a signed webhook (GitHub's signature works as is) starts a turn in its own thread. Webhooks can fill the prompt from their payload. Manage them in the project menu ▸ Triggers… or with `loom triggers`.
- **Slack and Discord:** mention the bot and the agent answers in the thread. The bot runs on your computer with your own tokens, so no public URL is needed. Only the people you list are heard. Set it up in Settings ▸ Slack & Discord, or with `loom bridge`.

**Work with a teammate**
- **Share live:** use the chat menu ▸ Share live…, or `loom share --live`. A teammate opens the link in a browser and joins that one chat, to read along, talk to the agent, or also approve its tool calls. Their messages carry their name, and access expires. They reach that chat and nothing else on your machine.
- **Audit trail:** `loom audit` shows who approved what, and what came in from teammates, Slack, Discord and triggers.
- **Search every session:** `loom find --all "…"` searches the chats in all your projects.

**Any agent, any view**
- **Any ACP agent:** `loom agents:add acp --preset gemini|claude|codex|opencode|grok` drives any Agent Client Protocol agent, with Loom's own permissions and approval cards.
- **Split workspace:** ⌘D splits side by side and ⌘⇧D one above the other. Drag a chat onto a pane's edge to open it there.
- **Instructions for a conversation:** standing guidance for one chat, sent with every turn in it.
- **`loom top`:** what each agent CLI, MCP server and dev server is costing this machine.

**Your phone, for every computer**
- **Every computer in one app:** pair the app with each computer you run Loom on, see them all live, and switch in one tap.
- **Allow / Deny on the notification:** answer from the lock screen, without opening the app.
- **Share live from the phone:** long-press a chat to invite a teammate.

**Also in this release**
- Each phone gets its own Loom Cloud key, so revoking one locks it out completely (since 1.2.8).
- The alerts webhook checks its secret in constant time.
- A queued message from a guest keeps their name.
- `loom chain` handles a run a worker already pushed (it fast-forwards and says "already on origin"), prints every project's outcome as it goes, and the planner can give a long task its own time limit.
