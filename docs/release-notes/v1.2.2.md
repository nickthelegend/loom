## Loom 1.2.2: Teams front and centre, and Loom that survives a restart

### Teams get their own tab, and you sign in with GitHub
- **A Team tab**, right after Chat. Teams used to sit at the bottom of Agents. Signed out, the tab shows what Loom Teams does and one **Sign in with GitHub** button. Once you're on a team, it shows:
  - members, with their GitHub pictures;
  - an **Invite** button;
  - who's working on what, and which files they're touching;
  - the team's activity and costs.
- **Loom Desktop asks you to sign in with GitHub** when it opens. "Continue without an account" is right there, since Loom works alone; the account is for Teams.
- **Invite works.** It said "admin only" in Desktop, because the window signed in like a phone. Desktop now holds the owner's session.
- **Accounts are GitHub-only**, enforced by the hosted database. An email sign-up could otherwise claim someone else's GitHub name.
- **Agents shows this project only.** Other projects are on the home screen.

### Survives a restart
- **Councils resume.** Use `loom council:resume`, or Resume on the card. A council picks up at the stage it stopped at, keeping its plan, reviews and decisions.
- **Night shifts stay in order after a restart.** A queued task is sent once and never on top of a running turn. The report waits until the work is really done.
- **`loom up --restart` leaves Loom Desktop's daemon alone**, and windows refresh themselves when the daemon restarts.

### Honest costs and budgets
- **The cold-session warning prices what you'll really pay.** It learns from that agent's own past turns: it said $0.34 where the real cost was $6.03, and now it says about $4.80. In a terminal it asks before sending.
- **A turn that would cross an agent's daily budget doesn't start.**

### Crews and reviews
- **Crews:**
  - Fixing a merge conflict no longer uses up a card's review rounds.
  - A failed card has **Accept it anyway**: its work is merged as it is, and the cards waiting on it run.
- **Reviews:**
  - A review waits for a busy reviewer instead of failing.
  - A reviewer that has never finished one is named up front.
  - A reviewer that runs out of time is stopped, process and all.

### Smaller things
- A shift or council starting on uncommitted edits tells you, and points to Rewind.
- Files an agent changed through the shell are counted as edits.
- `loom memory:repair` fixes decisions recorded before 1.2.1 with their underscores stripped.
- `loom clients --prune` clears out paired devices that never came back.
- `loom projects --json` and `loom status --json` exist, and `loom watch --json` is clean JSON.
- `loom doctor` and `loom agents` no longer call a signed-out agent ready.
- The window's "N working" matches `loom status`.
