Loom privacy policy

For the Loom phone app (dev.loom.app) and Loom itself. Last updated 9 October 2026.

Loom is open source (github.com/nickthelegend/loom). It runs on your own computer; the phone app is a remote for that computer. There are no Loom servers holding your code, chats or projects, and the app has no ads and no tracking.

What stays on your devices

  • Your projects, chats with agents, memories, diffs and files live on your computer, in the project folders you open with Loom.
  • The phone keeps the address of your computer and the pairing token it was given, in the phone's secure storage (Keychain / Android Keystore). Unpairing deletes them.
  • Pictures you attach from the phone are sent to your computer only, and saved in that project's .loom/attachments folder.

What leaves your devices, and only when you use it

  • Your computer. The app talks to the Loom running on your computer over your own network, your tailnet, or — if you turned it on there — Loom Cloud, an encrypted relay that passes messages through without being able to read them.
  • Notifications. If you allow notifications, the phone gets a push token from Google (Firebase Cloud Messaging) or Apple, via Expo's push service, and gives it to your computer. Your computer then sends the notification text (an agent's question, the start of a reply, a goal's outcome) through Expo and Google/Apple to your phone. Turn any kind off in the app's account sheet, or all of them in your phone's settings.
  • Voice input. Dictation uses your phone's own speech recognizer, which may send the audio to Google or Apple to turn it into text, under their privacy terms.
  • Your agents. What you send an agent (Claude Code, Codex, OpenCode and others) goes from your computer to that agent's provider, under your own account with them. Loom doesn't add anything of its own.
  • Update checks. Your computer asks GitHub for the newest Loom release every few hours.

Permissions the app asks for

  • Camera — to scan the pairing QR code, and to take a picture to attach. Nothing is recorded otherwise.
  • Microphone — only while you dictate a message.
  • Notifications — for the alerts above.

Accounts and analytics

The Play Store build has no sign-in and sends no analytics. Builds that offer the optional "Continue with Google" sign-in (for Loom's cloud features) keep your name and email with that sign-in, and may send, at most once a day and only if you leave it on, the phone's region setting, platform and app version — no device id, location or IP lookup.

Children

Loom is a developer tool and isn't directed at children.

Deleting your data

Unpair or uninstall the app to remove what it stored on the phone. Everything else is in your project folders and Loom's folder (~/.loom) on your computer — delete them there.

Contact

Questions or requests: open an issue at github.com/nickthelegend/loom/issues.
